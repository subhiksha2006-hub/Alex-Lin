// BACKGROUND & ETHOS

Architecting systems with craft, restraint, and tactile precision.

My path began over a decade ago dissecting browser markup—hand-coding bespoke Neopets fansites and personal web shrines before I even understood what a DOM tree was. That early fascination with digital agency grew into rigorous training at UC Berkeley, exploring where electrical engineering and software infrastructure converge with human sensory design.

Today, my work spans leading product interface engineering for Berkeley Mobile (reaching 40,000+ students daily), mentoring hundreds of budding hackers across Northern California through Cal Hacks, and teaching fundamentals as an undergraduate EECS instructor.
